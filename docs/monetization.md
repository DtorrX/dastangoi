# Guerrilla Profitability Playbook

## Niche positioning
- Compliance teams monitoring geopolitical risk.
- Investigative journalists and fact-checking orgs.
- NGOs and diaspora orgs tracking narrative shifts.

## Pricing suggestions
- Free: weekly digest, limited sources.
- Paid ($49/mo): daily digest + filters + PDF export.
- Pro ($199/mo): Slack webhooks + CSV/JSON + watchlists.

## Onboarding & retention
- Provide curated starter watchlists.
- Show confidence + rationale to build trust.
- Weekly “wins” email with notable catches.

## Risk/disclaimer language
- “Signals only; not verification.”
- “Public sources only; no private content.”
- “Human review required before action.”
